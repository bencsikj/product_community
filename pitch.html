The problem

We're more connected than ever, and still drifting apart.

~1 in 2

US adults report feeling lonely. Loneliness is now treated as a public-health issue, not just a personal one.

Source: U.S. Surgeon General Advisory, Our Epidemic of Loneliness and Isolation, 2023

We forget, and it isn't for lack of caring.

  • Life gets busy and months slip by
  • Small details (a new job, a big move) get lost
  • Reaching out after a long silence feels awkward

Finding help is scattered and one-way.

  • Mentors, skills, trainings and volunteering are spread across LinkedIn, Meetup, Slack and word of mouth
  • People are cast as "takers" or "givers"
  • Generosity goes unseen, so it doesn't last
Problem statement

People want to be good friends, colleagues and neighbors, but no tool helps them care and connect in one place.

What makes it different

Designed to feel like care, not a CRM.

Calm, not guilt

No red badges or "37 overdue follow-ups". Three small possibilities and nothing to catch up on.

Private by default

Notes are only for you. You choose what the community sees: your offers, asks and circles.

Give & take

Every profile has offers and asks. Matching runs both ways, so nobody is just a taker.

Real-world first

Walks, breakfasts and workshops. Warm intros over cold DMs.

How we'll know it's working

Measure care, not clicks.

Success metrics

  • Weekly care actions: reach-outs, notes, moments logged
  • Reciprocity: % of members with at least one offer and one ask
  • Match acceptance rate in the marketplace
  • Lasting ties: 2+ interactions within 60 days
  • Gathering attendance

Sustainable model (to explore)

  • Free core for individuals: your people and the community
  • Circle Host tools: RSVPs, rotating hosts, member insights
  • Partners: nonprofits, libraries and employers sponsoring mentoring and volunteer programs
  • Never selling personal notes or data
